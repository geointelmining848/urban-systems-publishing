# Rights and scholarly sources

No open-source license grant is specified for this repository. Public visibility alone
does not provide such a grant; permission for reuse is a matter for the rights holder.

Linked papers and journal guidance are the work of their respective authors and publishers.
Keep appropriate scholarly citations and follow the terms of the source material.
Links and attribution do not grant rights to reproduce copyrighted works. This package
does not include publisher abstracts, research datasets or manuscripts.

The optional corpus tools retrieve metadata from OpenAlex only when explicitly run.
Check the applicable API, metadata, and source-content terms before collecting or
redistributing records, especially abstracts. No API key is supplied. Python packages
and CI actions are external dependencies with their own licenses; none are vendored.
